About OriginChain

Blockchain-backed quality assurance and authenticity for luxury cosmetics

OriginChain follows one fictional luxury product, the Aurelia Prestige Renewal Serum, from raw-material receipt to the retail shelf. Every hand-off is quality-gated, and its fingerprint is recorded on a local blockchain so anyone can later check that the product is genuine and untampered.

Who does what

  • Supplier: registers raw-material batches and completes incoming QC with a Certificate of Analysis.
  • Manufacturer: links approved materials, registers the finished batch on-chain, completes manufacturing QC, transfers to the distributor.
  • Distributor: receiving, storage, damage and custody checks, then transfers to the retailer.
  • Retailer: the final gate; records Approved for Sale or puts the batch on Hold.
  • Admin: read-only oversight, audit log, issuer trust, and demo controls. Admin cannot act as a supply-chain party.
  • Consumer: no sign-in; verifies a product code and sees only public information.

Detailed records stay in an off-chain database; only compact hashes and custody events go on-chain. Comparing the two later reveals any tampering.

  1. 1
    Sign in with a role

    Each actor gets a short-lived token. The server checks role, product custody and stage on every action.

    FastAPI · Argon2 passwords · JWT · server-side RBAC
  2. 2
    Register & fingerprint

    Product metadata and ingredient lineage are hashed and committed to the smart contract when the batch is registered.

    Keccak-256 · OriginChain.sol on Hardhat · Web3.py
  3. 3
    Inspect each stage

    Checks come from reusable templates mapped to a versioned product specification and official source references.

    Quality templates · spec V1 · source registry
  4. 4
    Attach evidence

    PDF/PNG/JPEG evidence is stored off-chain; its byte hash is recorded on-chain. Critical reports also need a trusted issuer.

    5 MiB limit · integrity: Verified / Mismatch / Missing
  5. 5
    Pass the gate, hand over custody

    A stage can only be approved when every required check passes with valid evidence. Transfers re-check live status.

    Approval gates · ownership transfer events
  6. 6
    Verify publicly

    The consumer view recomputes the product hash and compares it with the blockchain. Any edit shows up as Suspicious.

    Hash comparison · evidence redaction

What this is

A local, single-machine educational demonstration built for a university project. The product, companies, inspection results and evidence are fictional. Source references (CDSCO Cosmetics Rules 2020, BIS and ISO standards) are real metadata used only to explain why evidence is requested.

What it does not claim

  • No ISO, BIS, CDSCO or FDA certification or approval.
  • No legal-compliance or laboratory-validity determination.
  • "Verified for Demo" means accepted by local workflow controls, not accredited.

Not production-ready

Shared demo passwords, a browser-stored token, SQLite, local evidence files and Hardhat test keys are demo-only. There is no MFA, rate limiting, TLS or production key custody.
