    About OriginChain

    Blockchain-backed quality and authenticity for luxury cosmetics

    OriginChain follows one fictional serum from raw materials to the shop shelf. Every hand-off must pass five quality checks, and each step is fingerprinted on a blockchain so anyone can confirm the product is genuine.

    4Stages
    5Checks per stage
    —Blockchain
    —Current block

    Every stage, the same five checks

    • Check Two hands-on inspections.
    • Certificate Two uploaded documents, fingerprinted on-chain.
    • Final review One sign-off that releases the batch.
    1. 1
      Sign in with a role

      The server checks your role and who holds the product before every action.

    2. 2
      Fingerprint the batch

      Product details are hashed (Keccak-256) and the hash is written to the smart contract.

    3. 3
      Pass five checks

      Certificates are hashed too. A failed check puts the product on HOLD.

    4. 4
      Hand over custody

      Only an approved stage can pass the product on; the transfer is recorded on-chain.

    5. 5
      Verify

      We re-hash our records and compare with the blockchain. Any edit shows as Suspicious.
